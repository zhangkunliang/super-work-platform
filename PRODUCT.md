# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui, as specified by the user.

## Users

Deep thinkers, creators, and independent knowledge workers seeking a focused digital environment.

## Product Purpose

Velorah presents a calm, cinematic entry point for a digital studio centered on focus and inspired work. This first surface should establish the brand and invite visitors to begin a journey.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page fullscreen hero.
- Fullscreen looping background video supplied by the user.
- Responsive glass navigation and two presentation-only "Begin Journey" calls to action.
- No decorative blobs, radial gradients, or visual overlays; the video supplies the visual depth.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Velorah.
- Voice: concise, reflective, and cinematic.
- User-supplied copy, video, typography, color tokens, glass treatment, and entrance animation are binding.

## Evidence on Hand

The user supplied the final hero copy and a CloudFront-hosted MP4. No testimonials, performance claims, or customer evidence were supplied and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Keep the first viewport focused on one memorable statement.
- Let real motion and imagery carry atmosphere.
- Preserve legibility and interaction clarity across viewport sizes.
- Keep presentation restrained and free of decorative filler.
